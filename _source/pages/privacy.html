Privacy

Updated November 2026

This site uses Metricool to count visits: which pages get read and where visitors came from, such as a search or an Instagram post. It helps me write more of what's useful. It doesn't use advertising trackers, and I don't use it to identify you.

When you fill in the "Tell me about your trip" form, it's hosted by Fora Travel, and your answers go to me so I can follow up. When you sign up for the newsletter, your email address is held by Substack, which sends it. You can unsubscribe from any issue.

I use what you tell me only to plan your trip and stay in touch about it. I share details with hotels, cruise lines and other suppliers only when they need them for your booking.
